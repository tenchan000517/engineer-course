## 3. Actual Procedures: Pro Workflow

To create "Top Musician Level" tracks, professionals follow this iterative workflow rather than one-shot generation.

### Step 1: Concept & Metatagging

Do not just type "cool rock song". Structure your lyrics with precise metatags to control the flow.

**Example Structure:**
```
[Intro: Slow melodic piano build-up]
[Verse 1]
(Lyrics...)
[Pre-Chorus: Rising tension]
[Chorus: Powerful, anthemic, multi-layer vocals]
(Lyrics...)
[Instrumental Break: Heavy bass line]
```

### Step 2: "Seeding" & Prompt Engineering

- **Style Prompt**: Be specific. Instead of "Jazz", use: "1950s Cool Jazz, quartet, brushed drums, double bass, smoky saxophone, warm production, vinyl crackle"
- **Audio Input (Optional)**: Record a humming melody or a rough guitar chord progression on your phone and upload it. This guarantees the melody follows your vision, not random chance.

### Step 3: Iterative Generation (The "Extend" Method)

1. **Generate the First Section**: Focus ONLY on getting a perfect Intro and Verse 1. (Generate 10+ takes if needed)
2. **Select & Extend**: Pick the best version. click "Extend"
3. **Prompt the Next Section**: In the "Extend" window, change the prompt if the energy needs to shift (e.g., add "Explosive drums" for the Chorus)
4. **Repeat**: Build the song block-by-block (Chorus -> Verse 2 -> Bridge -> Outro). This gives you granular control over every 30-60 seconds

### Step 4: Post-Production (The "Pro" Difference)

1. **Export Stems**: Download the "Pro Stems" (Vocals, Drums, Bass, Instruments, FX)
2. **External DAW**: Import stems into Logic Pro / Ableton / FL Studio
3. **Polish**:
   - **Vocals**: Apply pitch correction (Melodyne/Auto-Tune) to polish the AI vocals
   - **Dynamics**: Compress the drums and bass to make them punchier (AI mixes can be flat)
   - **Mastering**: Run the final mix through a mastering chain (Ozone) or a mastering service to reach competitive loudness (-9 LUFS or louder)

### The YouTube/X "Pro" Workflow

#### Phase 1: Advanced Prompting (The "X" Thread Method)

Community "Prompt Engineers" on X recommend a specific block structure:

- **Style Block**: `[Genre: 1980s Japanese City Pop] [Vibe: Nostalgic, Night Drive] [Instruments: Fender Rhodes, Slap Bass, Brass Section]`
- **Meta-Tagging Structure**:
  - `[Intro: Drum Fill]` -> sets the energy immediately
  - `[Verse 1 (Whispered)]` -> specific vocal direction
  - `[Chorus (Anthemic, Multi-tracked)]` -> widens the stereo image

**User Tip**: "Never use the word 'AI' in your prompt; it makes the model generic."

#### Phase 2: The "Extend" Chain (YouTube Technique)

1. **Seed the Idea**: Generate 10-20 seconds of just a riff or hook to lock in the "Voice"
2. **Extension Loops**:
   - Take the best 20s clip -> Click Extend
   - Change Prompt for the next section (e.g., add "Heavy Drums" for the Chorus)
   - Generate 0:20 to 1:00
   - Repeat until the song is finished
3. **Vocal Comping**: Generate multiple versions of the same verse and splice the best lines together

#### Phase 3: Post-Processing (The "Logic Pro" Step)

- **Stem Export**: Download separate tracks
- **Cleanup**: Use generic plugins to remove "AI Hiss" or artifacts from the high-end frequencies
- **Layering**: Add real instruments (e.g., a real guitar solo or live shaker) on top of the AI stems to fool the ear

### Community Tips & Tricks

| Feature | Community Usage / Tip |
|---------|----------------------|
| Custom Mode | Mandatory. "Simple Mode" is considered a toy by the community |
| BPM Control | Users report v5 ignores BPM often; tip is to write bpm like `[124 BPM]` inside the lyrics box as a meta-tag |
| Tags | Use `[Stop]` or `[End]` to force a clean ending, otherwise the AI often hallucinates a fade-out |
| Hallucination | If the AI sings gibberish, users suggest adding `(clear vocals)` or re-typing the lyrics in CAPS |
